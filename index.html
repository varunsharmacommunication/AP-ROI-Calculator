Methodology & assumptions

Current cost per invoice is calculated from your AP staffing cost divided by annual invoice volume, unless you supply your own known figure. Savings apply your chosen reduction percentage to that cost per invoice across your full volume. Capacity released converts the dollar savings into FTE-equivalent time, based on your loaded cost per FTE — it represents time freed for higher-value work, not a staffing recommendation. AstuteAP's GenAI Capture Engine delivers 95% extraction accuracy with average per-invoice data capture and coding in under 60 seconds; your own cycle time also depends on approval routing and exception volume, which is why manual and automated handling time above are editable. If no implementation investment is entered, a volume-based planning estimate is used and should be replaced with an actual quote.
